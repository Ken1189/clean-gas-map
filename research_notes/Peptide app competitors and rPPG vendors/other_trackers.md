# Competitive Landscape: Peptide and GLP-1 Tracker Apps (excluding Pep AI)

Research date: 27 Nov 2026. Method note: in this session, direct page fetches (WebFetch, curl to apps.apple.com, itunes.apple.com, peptideai.co, aletheahealth.ai, stacklogr.com) were blocked by the network proxy. All findings below come from search-engine result summaries of App Store / Google Play listings, vendor sites and third-party pages. Figures were not read off live store pages, so treat every rating count and price as a snapshot to re-verify. Almost every "best peptide tracker apps 2026" comparison is written by a competing vendor (Protocol, StackLogR, DoseTrack, LynkDose, Regimen, DoseMap, PepVault/PeptideMate, Pepti, GlucoPal, Shotsy). Independent evidence is limited to store ratings, app-analytics estimates (AppBrain, TrendApps, MWM/Spark) and user reviews.

## 1. Traction: which apps lead (ratings, downloads, revenue)?

### Takeaway
Shotsy is the clear category leader among GLP-1 trackers. It has tens of thousands of iOS ratings, a top-60 US Health & Fitness grossing rank and an estimated 130k+ Android downloads. Among peptide-specific trackers, traction is far smaller and fragmented. PeptidePal (about 4.8k to 4.9k iOS ratings) and Peptide Tracker by Flat Six Labs (about 1.9k to 3k ratings) lead. Most of the other names (Regimen, PepTracker, Glapp, Peptide AI, PeptIQ, Protocol, Peptify and others) have hundreds of ratings or fewer. Pep AI (out of scope) showed about 3.7k ratings in one snapshot, which puts it in the top tier.

- The "best of" posts are SEO competition between vendors. They are useful for features and prices, but rankings should be ignored.

## 2. Features, platforms, AI, pricing and privacy by app

### Takeaway
Core features have converged: dose logging, reminders, site rotation and side-effect logs are table stakes. Paywalls typically sit on estimated drug-level (half-life) charts, Health sync, unlimited compounds, AI and photos. Pricing clusters around $5–15 a month or $30–60 a year, with some lifetime options ($34.99–$79.99) and a few fully free or donation-funded apps. AI is mostly a chat companion or "stack analysis" that is disclaimed as not medical advice. Privacy positioning (on-device or iCloud only, "we never sell data") is a common differentiator.

### Cited Findings
**Shotsy**
- Platforms: iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android, in 17 languages (per a vendor comparison page). Widgets include medication level and dose countdown. — [Shotsy vs Glippy (vendor)](https://shotsyapp.com/alternatives/glippy/)
- Free core features with no time limit on iOS and Android: shot and dose logging, reminders, injection site rotation, side-effect logging (nausea, constipation, fatigue with severity) and weight. — [Shotsy free tracker](https://shotsyapp.com/free-glp-1-tracker/)
- Shotsy+ (paid) adds the estimated medication-level chart, which is based on published half-life data and described as "not a measurement", and Health Connect sync on Android. — [Shotsy GLP-1 tracker page](https://shotsyapp.com/glp-1-tracker/); [glp1.how](https://glp1.how/tools/shotsy)
- Pricing: about $9.99–$19.99 a month or $39.99–$59.99 a year (per comparison sites). It is GLP-1 only, with no peptide reconstitution calculator, and BPC-157 and TB-500 are not supported. It also supports oral GLP-1 pills. — [longeviters comparison](https://longeviters.com/apps/best-peptide-tracker-app); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/shotsy-glp-1-tracker/id6499510249)
- Privacy: "We never sell your data". Data is stored using Apple's private iCloud. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/shotsy-glp-1-tracker/id6499510249)

**PeptidePal (Blank Labs)** – iOS and Android. Subscription only, quoted as up to $14.99 a month or $59.99 a year, or $8.99–$9.99 a month or $29.99–$59.99 a year depending on source. — [LynkDose](https://lynkdose.com/blog/best-peptide-tracker-app/); [Pepteca](https://pepteca.com/peptidepal-review)

**Peptide Tracker (Flat Six Labs)** – iOS, Android and web. Fully free, with optional "Founding Member" tips of $5–$80. It has a reconstitution calculator and vial inventory. — [LynkDose](https://lynkdose.com/blog/best-peptide-tracker-app/); [peppytracker comparison](https://peppytracker.com/guides/best-peptide-tracker-app/)

**PepTracker** – a simple dose log for peptides, GLP-1s and testosterone. Pricing conflicts across sources: $4.99 a month, or $7.99 a month / $47.99 a year with lifetime from $79.99. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/peptracker-dose-log/id6747189889); [LynkDose](https://lynkdose.com/blog/best-peptide-tracker-app/)

Other reviewers called it "a little buggy" or "useless". — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/peptideai-peptide-tracker/id6760374374); [LynkDose](https://lynkdose.com/blog/best-peptide-tracker-app/)

**Other apps with listings (no traction data)**

### Inferences
- Feature parity is high. A safety-first entrant cannot differentiate on logging, calculators or half-life charts. Those are free or cheap almost everywhere.
- AI in this category is either a GLP-1 lifestyle or nutrition companion (MeAgain Capy, GLP AI) or a peptide "stack/cycle advisor" (Peptide AI, Smart Peptide Tracker Stack Analyzer, MAX Peptide AI). The second type effectively gives protocol advice for unapproved research compounds.
- Pricing anchors for the US market are about $40–60 a year. African pricing will need to be far lower, or use lifetime or local-currency options (see section 5).

### Gaps
- No feature data was found for Injex, PepDose, DoneDose, Peptocol, OptiPin, LynkDose, Peptide Stack Tracker or "PeptiPal".
- Account-versus-on-device models were not confirmed for most apps.
- I found no confirmed details on progress photos, apart from GLP AI paywalling them per reviews.

## 3. Safety features (triage, escalation, contraindications, verification, clinician sharing, double-dose)

### Takeaway
No app in the results offers symptom triage or red-flag escalation (for example, for pancreatitis, gallbladder disease or severe dehydration). Side-effect logging is passive: severity scales and notes. A third-party site claims one app (PeptIQ) has an interaction/contraindication checker, but this is unverified. No tracker integrates counterfeit or product verification; Janoshik COA checks exist only as separate web tools and guides. Clinician sharing exists mainly as B2B clinic consoles (PeptIQ). Several apps give or imply dosing protocols for unapproved research peptides. Every app reviewed disclaims medical advice. This leaves the safety-first position largely open.

### Cited Findings
- Search for red-flag or pancreatitis escalation in GLP-1 trackers found none. Apps such as Glapp and Shotsy offer severity-rated side-effect logs only. — [Glapp](https://glapp.io/); [Shotsy](https://shotsyapp.com/free-glp-1-tracker/)
- Pep AI and PeptIQ each state that they are tracking tools and not medical advice. MeAgain's Capy is "not a healthcare provider". — [pepaiapp.com](https://pepaiapp.com/); [PeptIQ App Store](https://apps.apple.com/mx/app/peptiq-peptide-tracker/id6757513095?l=en-GB&platform=vision); [MeAgain](https://meagain.com/glp-1-questions)
- PeptIQ: a third-party review site lists an "interaction checker that flags stacking conflicts and contraindications". I could not confirm this on the vendor's own pages. — [peptidetrackertracker PeptIQ](https://peptidetrackertracker.com/apps/peptiq)
- Clinician sharing: PeptIQ's provider console shows clinics adherence and labs. Shotsy markets its data as useful for insurer prior authorizations. — [PeptIQ clinics](https://peptiq.io/clinics); [GLP-1 Studio](https://glp1studio.substack.com/p/shotsy)
- Research-peptide dosing:
  - Peptide AI reviewers say it "recommended a clear, structured cycle based on their goals". — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/peptideai-peptide-tracker/id6760374374)
  - Smart Peptide Tracker scores stacks on "synergy... evidence quality". — [peptidetrackertracker](https://peptidetrackertracker.com/apps/smart-peptide-tracker)
  - Most peptide trackers ship reconstitution calculators for compounds such as BPC-157 and TB-500.
- Counterfeit and COA verification. Janoshik reports carry a task number and key, which can be checked on Janoshik's own verification page. Sources give conflicting URLs for that page, warn of phishing lookalike domains, and note that verification confirms the lab issued the report, not that the vendor's vial matches it. These guides are mostly written by peptide sellers. — [Durham Peptides](https://www.durhampeptides.ca/post/janoshik-verify-test-report-unique-key); [vantixbio](https://vantixbio.com/blog/janoshik-verification.html); [peptidecensus](https://peptidecensus.com/verify-coa/); [Peptabase COA tool](https://www.peptabase.com/coa); [PepPal blog](https://www.peppal.app/blog/janoshik)
- Double-dose detection: no evidence found. One review describes GLP AI *blocking* a second weekly shot log behind a paywall, which is a monetization gate, not a safety feature. — [GLP AI App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/glp-ai-glp-1-tracker-coach/id6755669956)

### Inferences
- A tracker that combines symptom triage and escalation (for example, severe persistent abdominal pain leading to an urgent-care prompt), contraindication screening (MTC/MEN2 history, pregnancy, pancreatitis history), duplicate-dose alerts, and pen and batch authenticity checks would have no direct equivalent among the apps found.
- Apps that hand out cycle protocols for unapproved peptides carry regulatory exposure. A safety-first app can position against them.

### Gaps
- I could not verify whether any app does PDF export for clinicians; the search returned nothing on this.
- Double-dose or minimum-interval warnings were not confirmed in any app. Many have reminders and could plausibly warn, but no source states it.

## 4. Vitals integration (Apple Health, Health Connect, wearables, BP/HR, camera rPPG)

### Takeaway
Integration is shallow. Several trackers sync weight, steps or active calories with Apple Health or Health Connect, often as a paid feature. None of the trackers found integrates blood pressure or heart rate as a monitored safety signal, and none uses camera-based rPPG vitals. rPPG vendors (Shen.AI) explicitly market a GLP-1 use case, but I found no consumer tracker deploying it.

### Cited Findings
- Glapp syncs weight from connected scales via Apple Health and Health Connect. — [glapp.io](https://glapp.io/)
- GlucoPal syncs steps and active calories. — [glucopal.com](https://glucopal.com/)
- Pep AI imports weight and steps. — [pepaiapp.com](https://pepaiapp.com/)
- GLPzy and OneGLP read Apple Health data but do not write back to it. — [glpzy.app](https://www.glpzy.app/); [oneglp.app](https://oneglp.app/)
- Shotsy+ includes Health Connect sync on Android. Smart Peptide Tracker's paid tier includes Health Connect sync. — [Shotsy](https://shotsyapp.com/glp-1-tracker/); [Smart Peptide Tracker Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.smartpeptidetracker.app&hl=en_US)
- Reviewers report GLP AI steps failing to sync with Apple Health, and Shot Pal syncing incorrectly with Apple Health. — [GLP AI](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/glp-1-tracker-diet-app-glp-ai/id6755669956); [Shot Pal](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/glp-1-tracker-shot-pal/id6739467364)
- Shen.AI markets GLP-1 programs as a use case. Its pitch: scans before prescribing and at each dose step, with heart-rate and BP cohort trends, citing heart-rate monitoring recommendations in GLP-1 labeling. This is vendor material; details are covered by another researcher. Thryve offers a middleware integration. — [Shen.AI weight management](https://shen.ai/use-cases/weight-management); [Thryve](https://www.thryve.health/features/connections/shen-ai-integration)

### Inferences
- Camera-based HR and BP checks at dose escalation would be a genuine first among consumer GLP-1 and peptide trackers, based on what surfaced. This is especially relevant in African markets with low wearable penetration.

### Gaps
- I found no evidence of any tracker reading BP cuffs or HR from wearables as safety signals. Absence in search results is not proof of absence.
- No evidence was found of Binah.ai or NuraLogix integrations in any tracker.

## 5. Africa availability and localisation; Africa-built competitors and telehealth

### Takeaway
I found no Africa-built peptide tracker. One South African GLP-1 tracker (GLP Wise) is positioned locally: POPIA-compliant, priced in rand, R999 lifetime. South Africa and Nigeria have telehealth GLP-1 prescribing services; Kenya has new semaglutide launches. In Zimbabwe, regulators have warned against illegal Ozempic use, and an unofficial source says Ozempic is not MCAZ-registered (as of Jan 2026). Counterfeit risk is documented in SA and flagged in Zimbabwe. Shotsy's 17 languages are the strongest localisation among the global apps, but store availability in ZW/ZA/NG could not be verified.

### Inferences
- Zimbabwe's demand is real but largely grey-market. A safety-first app with counterfeit and pen checks, escalation and MCAZ-aligned messaging fits the regulator's stated concerns. However, giving dosing support for an unregistered product could create regulatory friction.
- The pharmacy and telehealth partners in SA (Online Doctor SA, Get-Tested) and Nigeria (Kansei) are potential distribution channels or competitors for any "Africa next" expansion.
- Global apps price in USD at $40–60 a year. Local-currency and lifetime pricing (GLP Wise's R999, about US$55) is the only Africa-specific pricing model seen.

### Gaps
- I could not verify App Store or Google Play availability of Shotsy, Glapp, PeptidePal and others in the ZA, ZW or NG storefronts; store pages were not reachable. Most US-developed apps are typically available worldwide unless restricted, but this is unconfirmed.
- I found no African-language (Shona, Ndebele, Zulu, Yoruba and others) support in any app.
- No Zimbabwe-built health apps in this space were found.

## 6. Common user complaints across the category

### Takeaway
Store reviews repeatedly cite four problems: sudden paywalls on previously free features (including history), data loss or sync failures with iCloud and Apple Health, billing problems, and bugs or duplicate entries. Reddit threads did not surface in searches, so community sentiment there is unverified.

### Cited Findings
- **Paywall shifts:**
  - GLPTracker users report that a pop-up suddenly demanded $9 a month to view history. — [GLPTracker reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/glp-1-tracker-glptracker/id6755542893?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone)
  - A Glapp reviewer says "nearly everything is paywalled... overnight"; the developer replied that subscriptions fund features. — [Glapp Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.glapp&hl=en_NZ)
  - GLP AI reviewers regret the yearly plan and report paywalled progress photos and a paywalled second-shot log. — [GLP AI](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/glp-ai-glp-1-tracker-coach/id6755669956)
  - Shotwise locks all features behind a subscription. — [Shotwise](https://apps.apple.com/py/app/glp-1-tracker-shotwise/id6740042404)
- **Data loss and sync:**
  - A Jabby reviewer lost a year of dose and weight data despite iCloud; a later update restored it. — [Jabby](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/jabby-glp-1-medication-tracker/id6746419318)
  - Shot Pal reviewers report glitches and incorrect syncing with Apple Health. — [Shot Pal](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/glp-1-tracker-shot-pal/id6739467364)
  - GLP AI reviewers report steps not syncing, duplicated food logs, and barcode scans that doubled calories. — [GLP AI](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/glp-1-tracker-diet-app-glp-ai/id6755669956)
- **Billing and data loss in Pep AI (context):** complaints about billing and data loss appear on Pep AI's listing. — [Pep AI](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pep-ai/id6758682710)
- **Bugginess of AI peptide apps:** Peptide AI reviewers called it "a little buggy" or "useless". — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/peptideai-peptide-tracker/id6760374374)
- **Weekly subscriptions:** some apps (Shotly, Glapp Max) offer weekly subscriptions, a pattern often criticised as predatory. — [Shotly](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6755091838); [LynkDose](https://lynkdose.com/blog/best-peptide-tracker-app/)

### Inferences
- Trust levers matter in this category: never paywall a user's own history, offer reliable export and backup, and price transparently. That matters more for low-bandwidth, price-sensitive African users, where offline-first, on-device storage with optional encrypted backup addresses both data-loss and privacy concerns.

### Gaps
- No Reddit (r/Peptides, r/Semaglutide, r/Mounjaro, r/tirzepatide) threads surfaced via search, and Reddit was not directly fetchable. Community app preferences and complaints from Reddit are unverified.
- Review samples are small and anecdotal, with no quantitative complaint analysis.
