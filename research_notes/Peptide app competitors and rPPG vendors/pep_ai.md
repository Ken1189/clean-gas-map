# Pep AI: Peptide GLP-1 Tracker (competitor profile)

Research method note (read first): the session could not open apps.apple.com or play.google.com directly (DNS blocked by the session network policy), and per policy no further direct page fetches were attempted. Everything below comes from search-engine result snippets and summaries of the App Store, Google Play, pepaiapp.com and third-party pages. Exact figures (prices, rating counts, privacy labels) should be re-checked against the live store pages before they are quoted in a final report. Research date: 27 Nov 2026. The app has been renamed several times: "Pep AI - Peptide Tracker", "Pep AI: Peptide GLP-1 Tracker", "Pep AI: GLP-1 Peptide Tracker", "Pep AI: GLP-1 Tracker App". All of these share App Store ID 6758682710 and the Play package app.replit.pepai.

Several apps have similar names and should not be mixed up with this one: "Peptide AI" (id6760367577, Peptide AI LLC / peptideai.co), "PeptideAI" (id6760374374), "Peptides AI" (owned by Longeviters), "PepAI: Smart Peptide Log" (id6771703417), "PepAI - Your Daily Companion" (Pepapp Teknoloji, a Turkish app), and "GLP-1 Tracker: Pep" (Shred Apps, com.shredapps.glp1, pepglp1.com). A comparison site has already confused Pep AI with the Shred Apps "Pep" app.

## 1. Who makes it, when it launched, and where it is available

### Takeaway
Cedric Roberge, a 23-year-old marketing student and first-time founder, built Pep AI largely on Replit ("vibe-coded", which explains the `app.replit.pepai` package name). The store seller is his entity, Zode Development LLC. The iOS version 1.0 came out on 17 Feb 2026 and Android followed. The App Store ID has been indexed on Ghana and many other storefronts. I found no evidence either way for South Africa or Zimbabwe.

### Cited Findings
- The App Store and Google Play list Zode Development LLC as developer/seller. Support is support@pepaiapp.com, and the privacy and terms pages are at pepaiapp.com/privacy and pepaiapp.com/terms. — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pep-ai/id6758682710); [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.replit.pepai)
- Cedric Roberge describes himself as "Founder & CEO of Pep AI" on LinkedIn. — [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/cedric-roberge-3832472bb/)

### Inferences
- The "individual vs. LLC" difference in developer names is almost certainly the founder (Roberge) versus his publishing entity (Zode Development LLC), not two separate organizations.
- Because a Ghana storefront page is indexed, the app is probably distributed worldwide by default, including in African storefronts. Availability in ZA and ZW is likely but has not been verified. The app is English-only (no localisation was seen), so there is no Africa-specific content.
- A single non-technical founder built the app on Replit in about a week. That suggests limited QA, clinical governance and security engineering, which fits the bug reports in section 4.

## 2. Full feature list

### Takeaway
Pep AI covers far more than dose logging. It includes inventory and vials, a reconstitution calculator with an interactive syringe, stacks and cycles with off-weeks, site rotation, GLP-1 brand support, side-effect, nutrition and meal-scan logging, check-ins, bloodwork, Apple Health / Health Connect sync, PDF reports, a 75–90+ compound "Research Library", and "Pep Bot" AI chat. I found nothing on the AI model behind it or on any vitals or triage features.

### Cited Findings
- App Store positioning: "the all-in-one peptide and GLP-1 tracking tool built to keep your inventory, logs, nutrition, wellness data, and notes in one place". Listed features include a peptide calculator for reconstitution math, an injection site tracker, a bloodwork tracker and "AI Insights". — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6758682710); [App Store (alt)](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6758682710)
- Per-medication dose schedules, injection-site rotation, a community section, and coverage of Zepbound, Wegovy, Ozempic, Mounjaro and other semaglutide and tirzepatide products. It generates PDF reports "to review with a healthcare provider". — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pep-ai-glp-1-peptide-tracker/id6758682710)
- The Research Library has "75+ peptide profiles with educational summaries and vial images". One comparison site says 90+ compounds. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6758682710); [optipin.app (competitor)](https://optipin.app/pep-ai-vs-peptide-tracker)
- The Google Play listing adds a peptide calculator, a research library, a sleep tracker, and a "half-life estimator" ("estimated half-life and peak timing"). — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.replit.pepai)
- Vendor reconstitution calculator: converts vial amount, dose and bacteriostatic water into units on a U-100 or U-40 syringe. The vendor says the app also remembers vials and doses, shows an interactive syringe, counts down the doses left in each vial, and sends reminders. The site has separate semaglutide, tirzepatide and BPC-157 calculator pages. — [pepaiapp.com/reconstitution-calculator](https://pepaiapp.com/reconstitution-calculator); [semaglutide](https://pepaiapp.com/semaglutide-reconstitution-calculator); [tirzepatide](https://pepaiapp.com/tirzepatide-reconstitution-calculator); [BPC-157 dose calculator](https://pepaiapp.com/bpc-157-dose-calculator); [calculators hub](https://pepaiapp.com/peptide-calculators)
- Stacks: "separate schedules and vials for each, one calendar, automatic reconstitution math and site rotation across the whole stack". Cycles: a custom cycle length and off-weeks for each compound. Protocol tracker: compounds, schedules, vials, reconstitution, injection sites, cycles and "observations". Vial tracker: concentration, doses left and vial age. — [stack tracker](https://pepaiapp.com/peptide-stack-tracker); [cycle tracker](https://pepaiapp.com/peptide-cycle-tracker); [protocol tracker](https://pepaiapp.com/peptide-protocol-tracker); [vial tracker](https://pepaiapp.com/peptide-vial-tracker)
- Longeviters' feature list: peptide calculator, inventory organisation, vial photos, injection site tracker, side-effect logging, nutrition logging, meal scanner, sleep tracker, Pep Bot, data export. Helloregimen says Pep AI "leads with an in-app AI chat and meal scanning". — [Longeviters (owns competing apps)](https://longeviters.com/apps/pep-ai-review); [Helloregimen (competitor)](https://helloregimen.com/blog/regimen-vs-pep-ai-2026)
- Daily check-ins cover energy, sleep and mood. Another review mentions hydration, progress photos and Apple Health sync. — [App Store GH](https://apps.apple.com/gh/app/pep-ai-peptide-glp-1-tracker/id6758682710); [Longeviters](https://longeviters.com/apps/pep-ai-review)
- Pep Bot, per the App Store: "answers general educational questions about peptides — not medical advice". Per the vendor site, it "answers with sources and never gives doses". Another vendor page (BPC-157) calls it an AI research assistant for questions about "compounds, cycles, and dosing windows". A third-party description says it can also "identify patterns across tracked data". — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.replit.pepai); [pepaiapp.com](https://pepaiapp.com/); [BPC-157 tracker page](https://pepaiapp.com/bpc-157-tracker); [Sofarbot](https://www.sofarbot.com/stories/pep-ai-cedric-roberge-30000-monthly-revenue)
- A competitor says Pep AI has a Watch app and community groups. — [optipin.app](https://optipin.app/pep-ai-vs-peptide-tracker)
- Onboarding has 27 steps and ends in a hard paywall with a free trial. AppLlama captured 41 screens across onboarding, paywall and core tabs. — [ScreensDesign](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/pep-ai-peptide-glp-1-tracker); [AppLlama](https://appllama.io/apps/6758682710/pep-ai-peptide-glp-1-tracker)
- Sources conflict on AI: optipin.app (a competitor) says Pep AI has "no AI features and no hormone modeling", but every other source, including the store listings, describes Pep Bot and AI Insights. — [optipin.app](https://optipin.app/pep-ai-vs-peptide-tracker)

### Inferences
- Pep AI already offers essentially every tracking feature a new entrant would plan. A Zimbabwe/Africa app cannot win on tracking breadth. It would have to compete on safety (triage, red flags, clinician escalation), local context (pricing, offline use, local brands and compounding realities, local languages) and trust.
- "Evidence grading" does not appear as a feature in any source. The Research Library gives "educational summaries", and the BPC-157 page says evidence is weak, but there is no evidence-level system (such as A/B/C).

### Gaps
- No source discloses which LLM Pep Bot uses (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.), what its system prompt is, or how it handles refusals. Exact guardrail behaviour would need hands-on testing.
- No evidence was found of camera/rPPG vitals (Shen.AI, Binah, etc.), blood-pressure or heart-rate capture, symptom triage, or red-flag escalation. Absence in search results is not proof of absence.
- The bloodwork tracker's details (manual entry vs. lab import, which markers it covers) were not found.

## 3. Pricing

### Takeaway
The app is free to download, but there is a hard paywall with a 3-day trial. Premium costs about US$9.99/month plus an annual plan whose price has moved between $29.99 and $49.99. Some snapshots show a $3.99/week plan. Prices vary by storefront and date, and the app has cycled through several annual price points.

### Cited Findings
- US App Store: "Pep AI Monthly $9.99" and "Pep AI Yearly $29.99", auto-renewing Premium. The Mexico storefront shows the same plans in local currency. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6758682710); [App Store MX](https://apps.apple.com/mx/app/pep-ai/id6758682710)
- "A paid subscription is required to access its tracking features." — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pep-ai-glp-1-peptide-tracker/id6758682710)
- Hard paywall with trial at the end of onboarding. — [ScreensDesign](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/pep-ai-peptide-glp-1-tracker)
- AppLlama shows $3.99/week and $29.99/year. Optipin shows $3.99/week and $29.99–$49.99/year. — [AppLlama](https://appllama.io/apps/6758682710/pep-ai-glp-1-peptide-tracker); [optipin.app](https://optipin.app/pep-ai-vs-peptide-tracker)
- Helloregimen: 3-day trial, then $9.99/month or $44.99/year. Its free tier is limited to the Research Library. — [Helloregimen (competitor)](https://helloregimen.com/blog/regimen-vs-pep-ai-2026)

- Conflict: some vendor SEO pages and one third-party source say core tracking (adding peptides, logging doses, basic reminders) is free with "no paywall", which contradicts the store listings and paywall captures. — [Longeviters search summary](https://longeviters.com/apps/pep-ai-review); [pepaiapp.com](https://pepaiapp.com/)

### Inferences
- In USD terms, $29.99–$49.99/year is cheap for US users but expensive for most Zimbabwean consumers, especially those without USD card or App Store billing. A lower local price, mobile-money payment, or a meaningful free safety tier could set an African entrant apart.
- The free-vs-paid wording varies across vendor pages. That inconsistency could be framed as a trust weakness.

### Gaps
- No ZAR, GHS or USD pricing was found for the African storefronts. Whether a weekly plan is still live as of Nov 2026 is unconfirmed.

## 5. Safety stance

### Takeaway
Pep AI positions itself as a non-medical "tracking and education" app and says it gives no dosing instructions. In practice its marketing heavily targets unapproved and research-grade peptides (BPC-157, TB-500-type compounds, GH secretagogues) with dedicated trackers and dose-to-syringe calculators. Its own BPC-157 page admits there is no established human dosing. I found no vitals capture, side-effect triage, or red-flag escalation.

### Cited Findings
- "Pep AI is not a medical app and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or dosing instructions." — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6758682710)
- Vendor site: "a tracking and education app, not a medical device". — [pepaiapp.com](https://pepaiapp.com/); [BPC-157 tracker](https://pepaiapp.com/bpc-157-tracker)
- Google Play: "for informational and record-keeping purposes only". The App Store says it is for "tracking, organization and educational purposes only". — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.replit.pepai); [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pep-ai-glp-1-peptide-tracker/id6758682710)
- Pep AI's own BPC-157 explainer says there is almost no human clinical evidence and no established dosing for human use. — [pepaiapp.com/what-is-bpc-157](https://pepaiapp.com/what-is-bpc-157)
- Yet the site runs SEO pages for a BPC-157 tracker ("doses, cycles & injection sites"), a BPC-157 dose calculator (mcg to syringe units), and an HGH peptide tracker ("GH secretagogues & releasers"). — [BPC-157 tracker](https://pepaiapp.com/bpc-157-tracker); [BPC-157 dose calculator](https://pepaiapp.com/bpc-157-dose-calculator); [HGH tracker](https://pepaiapp.com/hgh-peptide-tracker)
- On Pep Bot, the vendor site says it "never gives doses", while the BPC-157 page describes it as handling questions about "dosing windows". — [pepaiapp.com](https://pepaiapp.com/); [BPC-157 tracker](https://pepaiapp.com/bpc-157-tracker)
- Side-effect logging exists, according to a third-party review. No source describes thresholds, alerts, or advice to seek care. — [Longeviters](https://longeviters.com/apps/pep-ai-review)

### Inferences
- The disclaimer-plus-calculator pattern lets Pep AI avoid "dosing advice" while still enabling self-administration of unapproved injectables. A safety-first competitor can contrast itself directly: it could refuse or caveat calculators for unapproved compounds, grade evidence, and add red-flag escalation (for example pancreatitis or gallbladder symptoms on GLP-1s, injection-site infection, hypoglycaemia).
- Pep AI does not appear to account for medicine sourcing or counterfeit risk (a major issue in African markets).

### Gaps
- How Pep Bot actually responds to "what dose of BPC-157 should I take?" is unknown without hands-on testing. No published regulator actions (FDA, Apple removal) against Pep AI were found.

## 6. Data and privacy

### Takeaway
There are conflicting signals. The vendor says data is stored on-device or in iCloud, never sold, not shared, and not used to train models. The App Store nutrition labels, by contrast, have at times declared Usage Data and Identifiers (including Device ID) as "used to track you", and one version listed third-party advertising. Apple has not verified these labels.

### Cited Findings
- The listing says data is stored on the device and synced across devices via iCloud, and the developer does not sell data. — [App Store KZ](https://apps.apple.com/kz/app/pep-ai/id6758682710); [App Store TR](https://apps.apple.com/tr/app/pep-ai-peptide-tracker/id6758682710)
- Google Play data safety (per search summaries): no data shared with third parties, data encrypted in transit, users can request deletion. — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.replit.pepai&hl=en)
- App Store privacy labels: Usage Data used to track across other companies' apps and sites, and Identifiers / Device ID in some versions; one version lists Device ID for third-party advertising. Usage Data also appears under "Data Linked to You". — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pep-ai-peptide-glp-1-tracker/id6758682710); [App Store GH](https://apps.apple.com/gh/app/pep-ai-peptide-glp-1-tracker/id6758682710)
- A third-party write-up says the policy covers sensitive data (compounds, doses, side effects), and that the current policy states personal health data is not sold or used to train Pep AI-owned models. — [Sofarbot](https://www.sofarbot.com/stories/pep-ai-cedric-roberge-30000-monthly-revenue)
- The founder disclosed scaling with Meta ads ("before meta: $80k/month, after meta: $160k/month"), which fits the presence of tracking identifiers in the labels. — [Roberge on X](https://x.com/Cedric_Roberge/status/2065546899885904331?lang=en)

### Inferences
- Meta ad attribution SDKs plus "not used to train *Pep AI-owned* models" (which leaves third-party LLM processing open) is a weak spot. A competitor can stand out by having no ad trackers, local data residency, and an explicit LLM data-processing disclosure that also addresses Zimbabwe's Cyber and Data Protection Act.
- Android users report "constant syncing" and broken iPhone-to-Android migration. That suggests some cloud account or backend exists, not just iCloud. Account requirements are unclear.

### Gaps
- The full text of pepaiapp.com/privacy and /terms could not be read (fetch blocked). Still unknown: the LLM sub-processor, the hosting provider (Replit, Supabase?), data retention, and whether an account or login is mandatory.

## 8. Device and vitals integrations

### Takeaway
Pep AI syncs weight, steps and workouts/activity with Apple Health (iOS) and Health Connect (Android). Sleep, energy and mood are entered manually in check-ins. I found no evidence of camera/rPPG vitals (Shen.AI, Binah or similar) or of any heart-rate or blood-pressure capture.

### Cited Findings
- The App Store says it can "sync steps, workouts, and activity data directly from Apple Health". The vendor FAQ says "Weight and steps sync with Apple Health so your progress updates automatically", and that weight and steps sync via Health Connect on Android. — [App Store LB](https://apps.apple.com/lb/app/pep-ai-peptide-tracker/id6758682710); [pepaiapp.com](https://pepaiapp.com/); [pepaiapp.com/peptide-tracker](https://pepaiapp.com/peptide-tracker)
- Sleep, energy and mood are tracked through daily check-ins rather than Health data. — [App Store GH](https://apps.apple.com/gh/app/pep-ai-peptide-glp-1-tracker/id6758682710)
- A competitor claims there is a Watch app (unverified). — [optipin.app](https://optipin.app/pep-ai-vs-peptide-tracker)

### Inferences
- Camera-based vitals combined with red-flag triage would be a clear feature Pep AI lacks. This matters especially in Africa, where wearable ownership is low and Android dominates.

### Gaps
- Whether heart rate, HRV or blood glucose are read from Apple Health is unconfirmed. No rPPG SDK was mentioned in any source.
